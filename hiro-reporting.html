Privacy Notice · Hiro Reporting

What this tool shows
Hiro Reporting displays aggregate recruitment metrics for the Sovos Talent Acquisition function. Data includes open requisition counts, hiring stages, time-to-fill averages by function, upcoming start dates by role and department, and monthly hiring velocity.

Personal data included
This report contains the names of Sovos hiring managers and recruiters in their professional capacity. No candidate personal data is included — no names, email addresses, CVs, or assessment results.

Legal basis
Processing is conducted under legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR) for internal workforce planning and recruitment management purposes.

Access controls
This report is accessible only to authenticated Sovos employees with an approved account. Access is role-based and limited to relevant business functions.

Data source & freshness
Data is sourced from Sovos SharePoint recruitment tracking lists and is embedded at the point of publication. The report is refreshed periodically and labelled with the data-as-of date.

Data location
Authentication is managed via Supabase (EU Frankfurt region). No candidate data is stored in this system. Report data is static and embedded in the page.

Your rights & contact
For questions about this report or to exercise your data rights, contact the Sovos Talent Acquisition team at anna.sidorska@sovos.com. For broader data privacy enquiries, contact the Sovos DPO.
